Chicken Avocado and Apple Caesar Salad + Primal Kitchen Discount Save Print Prep time 10 mins Cook time 35 mins Total time 45 mins Author: Brooke Weiss Recipe type: Salad Serves: 2 Ingredients 2 boneless skinless chicken breast 1 tbsp ghee salt and pepper 1 avocado, sliced 1 apple, sliced 1 bunch red leaf lettuce, chopped 2 tbsp goat cheese 5-6 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing Instructions Preheat oven to 425 degrees. Place a cast iron skillet on the stove over medium high heat and add the ghee. Season the chicken breast with salt and pepper and when the skillet is hot, add the two chicken breast. Brush the top of each chicken breast with 1 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing. Let cook for 5-6 mins. Flip the chicken breast and brush with another 1 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing. Let cooke for 5-6 mins. Place the skillet with the chicken breast in the oven for 25 minutes. While the chicken is baking, make the salad. Add the chopped red leaf lettuce to a large bowl and add the sliced avocado, apple, and goat cheese. Gently toss to mix. Remove the chicken breast from the oven and slice. Add the sliced chicken to the salad and drizzle 3-4 tbsp of Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing on top. Gently toss until the chicken and dressing is mixed in. Transfer the salad to two plates or bowls. Enjoy! 3.5.3239
2 boneless skinless chicken breast
1 tbsp ghee
salt and pepper
1 avocado, sliced
1 apple, sliced
1 bunch red leaf lettuce, chopped
2 tbsp goat cheese
5-6 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing
Preheat oven to 425 degrees. Place a cast iron skillet on the stove over medium high heat and add the ghee.
Season the chicken breast with salt and pepper and when the skillet is hot, add the two chicken breast. Brush the top of each chicken breast with 1 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing. Let cook for 5-6 mins. Flip the chicken breast and brush with another 1 tbsp Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing. Let cooke for 5-6 mins.
Place the skillet with the chicken breast in the oven for 25 minutes.
While the chicken is baking, make the salad. Add the chopped red leaf lettuce to a large bowl and add the sliced avocado, apple, and goat cheese. Gently toss to mix.
Remove the chicken breast from the oven and slice. Add the sliced chicken to the salad and drizzle 3-4 tbsp of Primal Kitchen Avocado Oil Caesar Dressing on top. Gently toss until the chicken and dressing is mixed in.
Transfer the salad to two plates or bowls. Enjoy!
