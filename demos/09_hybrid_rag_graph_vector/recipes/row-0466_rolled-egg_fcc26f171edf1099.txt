The last side dish which Kazuko demonstrated in the South Arm Community Kitchen is Rolled Egg.
Rolled Egg is a popular bento box item and great for lunch box.
Ingredients
4 eggs
Dashi bonito soup stock seasoning to taste (optional)
soy sauce to taste
Please note: the bonito flakes should not be in the photo
Source: Kazuko
Serves 4
Instructions
Season with a little dashi bonito soup stock seasoning if using.
Season with soy sauce to taste.
Use a paper towel to wipe off excess oil.
Add enough beaten egg to form a thin layer.
Swirl the egg to cover the pan.
Slide the rolled egg to one end and add more egg mixture and repeat the rolling.
