Recipe: Japanese Fruit Pie

Description: This is a pecan pie with a fruity twist. Coconut and plump raisins are stirred into a sweet pecan pie filling and baked. Serve with vanilla ice cream or freshly whipped cream.

Recipe category: ['Dessert']

Recipe cuisine: ['Japanese']

Yield: ['8', '9 - inch pie']

Ingredients:
- 1 (9 inch) unbaked pie shell
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 0.33329999446869 cup butter, melted
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon distilled white vinegar
- 0.5 cup chopped pecans
- 0.5 cup shredded coconut
- 0.5 cup raisins

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
2. In a medium mixing bowl combine eggs, butter or margarine, sugar, vanilla extract, and vinegar. Beat until smooth. Stir in pecans, coconut, and raisins. Pour mixture into pastry shell.
