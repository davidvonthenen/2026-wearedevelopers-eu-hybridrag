Description
This is a great, healthy alternative to something sweet that you might be craving. If you don’t have a lot of time for breakfast, this is something you can throw together in just a few minutes and be heading out the door shortly after! This is also a great snack on a hot day.
Ingredients
1 whole Apple, Cored, Peeled And Sliced
½ cups Applesauce
½ teaspoons Cinnamon
⅛ teaspoons Nutmeg
2 Tablespoons Honey
1-½ cup Almond Milk
4 whole Ice Cubes
Preparation
Place all ingredients into a blender. Put the lid on and blend until thoroughly mixed and smooth. Add more ice cubes if desired. Serve immediately.
*Note: Plain or vanilla yogurt can be substituted for the almond milk. If you do this just add more applesauce so the smoothie isn’t too thick.
