Recipe: Moroccan-Style Tilapia With Cumin, Mango and Cilantro Recipe

Description: Make and share this Moroccan-Style Tilapia With Cumin, Mango and Cilantro Recipe recipe from Food.com.

Recipe category: Moroccan

Yield: 4 serving(s)

Prep time: PT10M

Cook time: PT10M

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 4 (5 ounce) tilapia fillets
- salt & fresh ground pepper
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons ground coriander
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 mango, ripe and diced
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaf, fresh and chopped

Instructions:
1. Season both sides of tilapia fillets with salt and black pepper.
2. Rub cumin and coriander all over both sides of fillets.
3. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add tilapia fillets and cook 3 to 5 minutes per side, until fish is fork-tender.
4. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine mango and cilantro.
5. Top tilapia with mango mixture just before serving.
