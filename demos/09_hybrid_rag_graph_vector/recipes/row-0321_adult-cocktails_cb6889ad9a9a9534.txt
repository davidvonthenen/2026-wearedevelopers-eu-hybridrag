Recipe: Adult Cocktails

Yield: 1 serving

Ingredients:
- 1.5 oz gin
- .5 oz Campari
- .25 oz Cointreau
- 2 oz ruby red grapefruit juice
- .25 ounce lemon juice
- orange peel to garnish
- 2 ounces vodka
- 2 sprigs mint, plus more for garnish
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- .5 oz simple syrup

Instructions:
1. Combine ingredients and serve over ice. Per drink (will be making enough to fit in cookie jar/vase).
2. Muddle mint, combine with vodka, lime juice & simple syrup. Serve over ice. Per drink (will be making enough to fill container).
