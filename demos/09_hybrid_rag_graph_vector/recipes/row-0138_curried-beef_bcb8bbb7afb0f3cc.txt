Recipe: Curried Beef

Description: Delicious mix of flavors, and fairly quick to make, especially if you buy pre-cut beef strips.

Recipe category: Main Dish

Recipe cuisine: Asian

Yield: 6

Prep time: PT20M

Cook time: PT30M

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 pounds round steak (all visible fat removed, in 1/8" bite-size slices)
- 2 Tbs veggie oil
- 2 medium onions quartered
- 1 Garlic
- 4 teaspoons Curry powder
- 1 cup Tomato juice
- 1/2 cup Water
- 1.25 tsp salt
- 1/4 teaspoon Sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon Cinnamon

Instructions:
1. Heat 1 Tbs oil in large skillet, and cook onions, garlic, and curry powder 5 mins. Put mixture in a bowl. Heat the other Tbs oil in the skillet and cook steak until browned on all sides. Add onion mixture and all other ingredients, heat to boiling, then reduce heat to low and cover and simmer 5 mins. Serve over basmati rice. Servings: 6
