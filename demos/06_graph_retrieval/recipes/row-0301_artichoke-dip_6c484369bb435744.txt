Recipe: Artichoke Dip

Description: Three basic ingredients, 10 minutes, what could be easier?

Recipe category: Appetizers

Recipe cuisine: American

Yield: 4

Prep time: PT10M

Cook time: PT30M

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 1 can (12 oz) Artichoke hearts
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup Mayonnaise (light is ok)

Instructions:
1. Chop artickokes and mix with parmesan and mayo in a heat-safe bowl. Microwave on low heat or bake until bubbly. Serve with crackers or bread rounds.
