Recipe: Peppermint Chiller

Description: This refreshing cocktail is a great way to cool down on a hot summer day. Make sure to have it ready in the fridge ... you never know when guests will drop by!

Recipe category: Punches

Recipe cuisine: American

Yield: 12-16 8 ounce servings

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT1H

Ingredients:
- 2 cup(s) sugar
- 4 cup(s) fresh peppermint leaves, with stems
- 2 cup(s) lemon juice
- 2 cup(s) orange juice
- 2 cup(s) ginger ale

Instructions:
1. In a large saucepan, combine sugar with 6 cups of water. Bring to a boil over high heat.
2. Reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Add peppermint, remove pan from the heat, cover, and let the liquid steep for 1 hour.
4. In a large pitcher, combine the mint syrup, lemon juice, orange juice, and ginger ale.
5. Stir until well blended. Serve over crushed ice.
