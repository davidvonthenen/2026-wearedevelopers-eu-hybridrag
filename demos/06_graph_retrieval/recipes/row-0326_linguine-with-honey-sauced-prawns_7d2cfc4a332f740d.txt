Recipe: Linguine With Honey Sauced Prawns

Description: This is another recipe I found hanging around on a piece of paper...it's from the National Honey Board...hope you enjoy...you can replace the prawns with shrimp...that's what I did...

Recipe category: One Dish Meal

Yield: 2 serving(s)

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT10M

Total time: PT25M

Ingredients:
- 1/2 lb prawns, peeled and deveined
- 1/4 cup carrot, julienne
- 1/4 cup celery, sliced
- 1/4 cup green onion, sliced diagonally
- 1 1/2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1/8 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/8 teaspoon dried rosemary leaves, crushed
- 1/2 lb linguine, cooked and kept warm

Instructions:
1. Stir-fry prawns, carrots, celery, green onions and garlic in oil in large skillet over medium-high heat about 3 minutes or until prawns start to turn pink.
2. Combine remaining ingredients except pasta in small bowl; mix well.
3. Add to prawn mixture; stir-fry about 1 minute or until sauce thickens.
4. Serve over pasta.
