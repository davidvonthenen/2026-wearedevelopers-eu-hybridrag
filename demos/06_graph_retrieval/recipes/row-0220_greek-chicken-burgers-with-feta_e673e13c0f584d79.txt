Recipe: Greek Chicken Burgers With Feta

Description: Make and share this Greek Chicken Burgers With Feta recipe from Food.com.

Recipe category: Chicken

Yield: 4 serving(s)

Prep time: PT20M

Cook time: PT10M

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 1 lb ground chicken
- 1/2 cup dry breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped sun-dried tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil
- 3 teaspoons chopped fresh oregano
- salt and pepper
- 2 ounces crumbled feta cheese

Instructions:
1. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat, and lightly oil the grate.
2. Mix the chicken, bread crumbs, egg, lemon juice, sun-dried tomatoes, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper together in a bowl; form the mixture into 4 thin patties.
3. Divide the feta cheese between the 4 patties; place a portion of the feta cheese atop each patty and fold the chicken mixture around the cheese so that the cheese is in the center and seal well.
4. Cook on the preheated grill until no longer pink on the inside and the juices run clear, about 5 minutes per side. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read 165 degrees F (75 degrees C).
