Description
Easy, healthy ice cream made with frozen bananas, milk, caramel, vanilla extract, and cinnamon to make this a delicious treat.
Ingredients
3 Frozen Ripe Bananas
¼ cups Milk (regular, Almond, Coconut, Etc.)
⅓ cups Caramel
¼ teaspoons Ground Cinnamon
¼ teaspoons Pure Vanilla Extract
1 pinch Salt
Preparation
In a blender or food processor, add all ingredients and blend until smooth. Pour mixture into a 9×5 inch baking pan and freeze until frozen (about 1 hour).
Remove for freezer and scoop up for deliciousness! Add toppings if desired.
