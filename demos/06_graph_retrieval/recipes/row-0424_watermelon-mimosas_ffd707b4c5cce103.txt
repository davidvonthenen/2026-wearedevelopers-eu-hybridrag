Recipe: Watermelon Mimosas

Description: Watermelon Mimosas from Delish.com are the prettiest cocktails of the season.

Recipe category: ['brunch', 'cocktail']

Yield: 4 serving(s)

Prep time: PT10M

Cook time: PT0S

Total time: PT15M

Ingredients:
- 4 c. cubed watermelon
- Juice of 1 lime
- 2 tbsp. granulated sugar
- 1 bottle champagne or prosecco
- Mint, for garnish
- Watermelon wedges, for garnish

Instructions:
1. Blend together cubed watermelon, lime juice, and sugar in a blender. Strain mixture through a fine mesh sieve, discarding solids.
2. Fill champagne flutes halfway with watermelon mixture and top off with champagne. Garnish glasses with a few mint sprigs and a watermelon wedge.
