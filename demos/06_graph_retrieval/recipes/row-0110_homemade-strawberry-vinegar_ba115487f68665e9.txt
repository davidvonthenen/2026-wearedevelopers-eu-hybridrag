Recipe: Homemade Strawberry Vinegar

Description: Try this recipe for homemade vinegar with fresh strawberries. Fruit-infused vinegars brighten up any salad and they are quick and easy to prep as well.

Recipe category: ['Condiment', 'Ingredient']

Recipe cuisine: ['American']

Yield: 16

Prep time: PT10M

Total time: PT2890M

Ingredients:
- 0.33333334326744 cup fresh strawberries
- 1.5 cups white vinegar

Instructions:
1. Slice tops off strawberries; place strawberries in a colander. Rinse, pat dry, and slice.
2. Pour vinegar into a resealable jar; add strawberries. Allow fruit to infuse at room temperature for 2 days.
3. Transfer vinegar to a resealable bottle; strain strawberries if desired.
