Recipe: Mango Smoothie

Description: Make and share this Mango Smoothie recipe from Food.com.

Recipe category: Smoothies

Yield: 2 serving(s)

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT5M

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 2 ripe mangoes
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 3 egg whites

Instructions:
1. Peel mangos and cut in to smaller pieces.
2. Combine all ingredients in a blender.
3. Blend on high until smooth.
4. Enjoy!
