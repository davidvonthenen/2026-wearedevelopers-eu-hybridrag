Recipe: Creamy Mushroom Soup

Description: This is one of our family favorite soups, from Helen's Pantry. It's good with a roast beef sandwich. June recipe COOKBOOK RECIPE

Recipe category: Cream Soups

Recipe cuisine: American

Yield: Serves 2 to 4

Ingredients:
- 1/4 cup(s) onions, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoon(s) parsley
- 1 tablespoon(s) butter
- 1 1/2 cup(s) mushrooms, sliced
- 1 1/2 teaspoon(s) corn starch
- 1 teaspoon(s) beef bouillon
- 1/2 teaspoon(s) Worcestershire sauce
- 1/3 teaspoon(s) dry mustard
- pinch(es) pepper
- 1/2 cup(s) sour cream

Instructions:
1. Saute onions in butter, do not brown.
2. Stir in sliced mushrooms, cook until tender.
3. Add cornstarch, beef bouillon, worcestershire sauce, dry mustard and pepper.
4. Add sour cream.
5. Run through blender until smooth or use an immersion blender.
6. Top with chopped parsley.
