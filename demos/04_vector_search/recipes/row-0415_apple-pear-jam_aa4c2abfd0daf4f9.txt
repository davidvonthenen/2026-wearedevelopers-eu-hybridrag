Recipe: APPLE PEAR JAM

Description: CONDIMENT FOR CORNBREAD
JAM FOR BREAKFAST
APPETIZER SERVED OVER CREAM CHEESE AND CRACKERS

Recipe category: Appetizers

Recipe cuisine: AMERICAN

Yield: 6

Prep time: PT1H

Cook time: PT2H

Total time: PT3H

Ingredients:
- 2 cups APPLES Peeled, CORED AND DICED
- 1 cup PEARS Peeled, CORED AND DICED
- 6.5 cups Sugar
- 1/4 tsp CINNAMON
- 1/4 cup LEMON JUICE
- 6 oz LIQUID PECTIN

Instructions:
1. MASH APPLES AND PEARS (YOU CAN USE A FOOD PROCESSOR). ADD SUGAR AND CINNAMON, AND LEMON JUICE AND COOK UNTIL THIS BOILS. IMMEDIATELY ADD PECTIN AND LET MIXTURE COOK AT A ROLLING BOIL FOR 1 MINUTE. TRANSFER TO CLEAN HOT JARS, AND PUT JARS IN CANNING POT TO BOIL FOR 5 MINUTES. FYI: My Woman's Canning Group multiplied this recipe 5 x's and it turned out 27 pint jars at $3.00 each.
