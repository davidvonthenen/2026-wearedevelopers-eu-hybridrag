Recipe: Coconut Cookies

Description: Active time: 35 min Start to finish: 5 hr

Yield: Makes about 4 dozen cookies

Ingredients:
- 2 cups sweetened flaked coconut
- Ingredients for basic butter cookies
- 1/4 teaspoon almond extract
- Garnish: sweetened flaked coconut and coarse sugar

Instructions:
1. Pulse coconut in a food processor until finely ground.
2. Follow recipe for basic butter cookies, mixing in coconut and almond extract after adding flour. (To roll cookies into balls, see cooks' note, below.)
3. If garnishing, sprinkle cookies with flaked coconut and coarse sugar before baking.
