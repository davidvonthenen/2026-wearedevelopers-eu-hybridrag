Recipe: Eating Well's Sweet Garlic &quot; Mayonnaise&quot;

Description: From the original Eating Well magazine. This recipe is from 1990. I wanted to post it here so I don't lose it.
This sauce is thick and creamy and marvelous on vegetables or meats. It also makes a tasty sandwich spread. Cook time is actually processing in a blender.

Recipe category: Lactose Free

Yield: 1 1/2 cups, 6 serving(s)

Prep time: PT5M

Cook time: PT2M

Total time: PT7M

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup eating well's sweet garlic paste
- 1 cup olive oil
- salt and pepper
- lemon juice (optional)
- Dijon mustard (optional)

Instructions:
1. Place garlic puree in a blender. With the motor running, gradually drizzle in the oil in a very fine stream.
2. When the mixture is fully combined, add salt and pepper to taste.
3. Add lemon juice and/or Dijon mustard to taste, depending on what you plan to use the sauce for.
4. Keeps in the refrigerator for a week.
