Noodles With Mozzarella Cheese
add review
#61475
Just because it's easy doesn't mean it's not delicious! The fresh ingredients make all the difference.
ingredients
4 tablespoons olive oil 3 tomatoes, peeled and chopped 4 ounces mozzarella cheese, cubed 2 ounces Cheddar cheese, grated chopped fresh oregano salt and freshly ground black pepper 12 ounces noodles
directions
Heat the oil in a saucepan and add the tomatoes, Mozzarella, Cheddar and oregano; season to taste. Cover and simmer for 5 minutes or until cheese has melted. Meanwhile cook the noodles in boiling water, drain and place in a warm dish. Combine with the cheese mixture and serve.
