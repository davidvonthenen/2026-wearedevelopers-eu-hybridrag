Recipe: Crockpot Lemon Rosemary Chicken

Description: We are loving this simple and healthy Crockpot Lemon Rosemary Chicken Freezer Meal that you can easily freeze and then prepare in your slow cooker.

Recipe category: ['Slow Cooker']

Yield: ['4', '4 people']

Prep time: PT10M

Cook time: PT480M

Total time: PT490M

Ingredients:
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1 whole fresh lemon juiced
- 2 cloves fresh garlic crushed and minced
- 1 TBSP dried rosemary
- 1/4 tsp. salt/pepper - optional
- 4-6 whole boneless chicken breasts or skinless boneless chicken thighs (2 lbs.)

Instructions:
1. Salt and pepper both sides of chicken breasts
2. Place all ingredients except for chicken broth into a freezer safe bag.
3. Freeze.
4. When ready to cook thaw in the refrigerator overnight.
5. Place into the slow cooker with 1 cup of chicken broth and cook on low for 6-8 hours until done.

Crockpot Lemon Rosemary Chicken
Ingredients
1 cup chicken broth
1/2 cup olive oil
1 whole fresh lemon juiced
2 cloves fresh garlic crushed and minced
1 TBSP dried rosemary
1/4 tsp. salt/pepper - optional
4-6 whole boneless chicken breasts or skinless boneless chicken thighs (2 lbs.)
Instructions
Salt and pepper both sides of chicken breasts
Place all ingredients except for chicken broth into a freezer safe bag.
Freeze.
When ready to cook thaw in the refrigerator overnight.
Place into the slow cooker with 1 cup of chicken broth and cook on low for 6-8 hours until done.
