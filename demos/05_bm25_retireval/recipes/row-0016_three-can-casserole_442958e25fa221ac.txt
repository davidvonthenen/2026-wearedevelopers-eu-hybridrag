Recipe: Three Can Casserole

Description: It does not get much simpler than this. By using canned ingredients, the Green Bean Casserole can also be made in an RV Kitchen, or even in a camp site Dutch Oven.

Recipe category: Vegetable

Yield: 4 serving(s)

Prep time: PT5M

Cook time: PT35M

Total time: PT40M

Ingredients:
- 1 (14 1/2 ounce) can green beans, drained
- 1 (4 ounce) can mushrooms, and the liquid in the can
- 1 (10 3/4 ounce) can cream of mushroom soup (DO NOT dilute)

Instructions:
1. Drain the green beans, put everything into a casserole dish and bake covered in a 350&deg;F oven for 35 minutes.
2. Add salt and or pepper to taste (optional).
