Recipe: Banana Fritters with Spiced Glaze

Recipe category: dessert

Yield: 16 fritters

Prep time: P0Y0M0DT0H10M0.000S

Cook time: P0Y0M0DT0H15M0.000S

Total time: P0Y0M0DT0H25M0.000S

Ingredients:
- 3 eggs, beaten
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 3 cups baking mix
- 3 ripe bananas, diced
- 2 cups canola oil, for frying
- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice

Instructions:
1. For the fritters: In a large bowl, whisk the eggs with the milk and sugar until blended. Stir in the baking mix and bananas until just combined.
2. Heat the canola oil in a large, heavy-bottomed skillet over medium heat. Using a small ice cream scoop, drop the batter into the skillet. Fry in batches until golden brown and cooked through the center, 2 to 3 minutes per side. Remove from the oil and allow to drain on a wire cooling rack set over a baking sheet.
3. For the glaze: Whisk together the powdered sugar, 1/4 cup water and pumpkin pie spice.
4. While the fritters are still warm, pour the glaze over top. Allow the glaze to set up slightly before serving.
