Recipe: Apple Cinnamon Turnovers

Description: not set

Recipe category: Breakfast

Recipe cuisine: not set

Yield: 8

Prep time: PT30M

Cook time: PT30M

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 1 medium Apple peeled and chopped
- 1/2 cup Applesauce
- 3/4 teaspoon Cinnamon
- Dash Nutmeg
- 7 1/2 ounce can Refrigerated biscuits
- 1 tablespoon Butter melted
- 2 tablespoon Sugar

Instructions:
1. In bowl, combine apple, applesauce, 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon and nutmeg. Separate biscuits; roll out into 6 inch circle. Place on greased baking sheet. Place a heaping tablespoon of apple mixture in the center of each. Fold in half and pinch edges together with fork tines. Brush with butter. Combine sugar and remaining cinnamon; spinkle over tops. Bake at 400 degrees for 10 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
