Description
Fresh berries marinated in balsamic vinegar and sugar poured over frozen yogurt.
Ingredients
1 cup Sliced Strawberries
½ cups Sliced Blackberries, Sliced In Half
1 Tablespoon Good Quality Balsamic Vinegar
1 Tablespoon Sugar
Frozen Yogurt, For Serving
Preparation
Place the sliced strawberries and blackberries in a bowl big enough for mixing. Add the balsamic vinegar and sugar and stir to coat very well.
Refrigerate the berry mixture for 1 hour, stir, and then serve. The berries can marinate overnight without adversely affecting the texture. However, they are best eaten within 24 hours.
