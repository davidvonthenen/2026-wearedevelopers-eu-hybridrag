Ingredients:
3 slices orange
3 slices pineapple
3 or 4 ice cubes
2 oz gin
1/2 oz sweet vermouth
1/2 oz grenadine
Directions:
Muddle orange and pineapple slices in the bottom of a cocktail shaker. Add the ice and remaining ingredients. Shake vigorously. Strain into a cocktail glass.
