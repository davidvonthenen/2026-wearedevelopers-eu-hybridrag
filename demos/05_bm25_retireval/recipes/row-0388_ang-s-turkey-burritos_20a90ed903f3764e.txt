Recipe: Ang&#39;s Turkey Burritos

Description: Make easy ground turkey burritos for a lighter take on the beef dish, complete with sweet corn, black beans, and chiles in wheat tortillas.

Yield: ['8', '8 servings']

Prep time: PT20M

Cook time: PT20M

Total time: PT40M

Ingredients:
- 1 pound ground turkey
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 (15 ounce) can whole sweet corn, drained
- 1 (15 ounce) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (10 ounce) can diced tomatoes and green chile peppers, undrained
- 0.5 cup chopped red bell pepper, or to taste
- 2 teaspoons lime juice
- 0.75 teaspoon garlic powder
- 0.75 teaspoon chili powder
- 0.5 teaspoon ground cumin
- 8 (8 inch) whole wheat tortillas

Instructions:
1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir turkey and onion in the hot skillet until turkey is browned and no longer pink, 5 to 7 minutes. Add corn, black beans, tomatoes, red bell pepper, lime juice, garlic powder, chili powder, and cumin. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until flavors combine, 15 to 20 minutes.
2. Place tortillas on a microwave-safe plate. Heat in the microwave until warmed, 15 to 20 seconds. Divide turkey mixture over the warmed tortillas.

Ang's Turkey Burritos
Turkey burritos are a lighter version of our favorite beef burritos. My husband actually prefers these over the local Mexican restaurant! Optional toppings: lettuce, fresh spinach, avocado, salsa, tomatoes, black olives, fat-free sour cream, low-fat Cheddar cheese, etc.

Directions
Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir turkey and onion in the hot skillet until turkey is browned and no longer pink, 5 to 7 minutes. Add corn, black beans, tomatoes, red bell pepper, lime juice, garlic powder, chili powder, and cumin. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until flavors combine, 15 to 20 minutes.
Place tortillas on a microwave-safe plate. Heat in the microwave until warmed, 15 to 20 seconds. Divide turkey mixture over the warmed tortillas.
Cook's Notes:
Use any color bell pepper you prefer.
Cooked brown rice can also be added to the mixture.
