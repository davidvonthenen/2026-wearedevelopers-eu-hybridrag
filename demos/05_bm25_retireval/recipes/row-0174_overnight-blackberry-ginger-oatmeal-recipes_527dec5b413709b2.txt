Recipe: Overnight Blackberry Ginger Oatmeal

Yield: 1

Prep time: PT15M

Ingredients:
- 1 (6 ounce) low fat vanilla yogurt
- 2/3 cup regular old fashioned oats
- 2/3 cup milk
- 1/4 cup apricot preserves
- 1/2 tablespoon chia seed
- 1/2 tablespoon flaxseeds
- 2 teaspoons snipped crystallized ginger
- 1/2 cup fresh blackberries

Instructions:
1. In a medium bowl stir together the yogurt, oats, milk, apricot preserves, chia seeds, flaxseeds and ginger.
2. Fold in the fresh blackberries.
3. Divide oatmeal mixture equally between TWO half pint jars with lids.
4. Cover and chill overnight or up to 3 days in refrigerator.
5. Add garnish before serving.

Overnight Blackberry Ginger Oatmeal | Perfectly creamy with a little touch of fruit and a hint of ginger. This heart healthy breakfast is a great way to start your morning. Even if you sleep in you can still have a homemade breakfast. Simply pack and go!
Make up several different versions for busy weekends with overnight visitors. Pack in half pint mason jars and take them to the beach for a great mid day snack. If eating oatmeal cold isn’t your thing you can heat it in the microwave for a comfy warm breakfast.
Ingredients
1 (6 ounce) low fat vanilla yogurt
2/3 cup regular old fashioned oats
2/3 cup milk
1/4 cup apricot preserves
1/2 tablespoon chia seed
1/2 tablespoon flaxseeds
2 teaspoons snipped crystallized ginger
1/2 cup fresh blackberries
Instructions
In a medium bowl stir together the yogurt, oats, milk, apricot preserves, chia seeds, flaxseeds and ginger.
Fold in the fresh blackberries.
Divide oatmeal mixture equally between TWO half pint jars with lids.
Cover and chill overnight or up to 3 days in refrigerator.
Add garnish before serving.
