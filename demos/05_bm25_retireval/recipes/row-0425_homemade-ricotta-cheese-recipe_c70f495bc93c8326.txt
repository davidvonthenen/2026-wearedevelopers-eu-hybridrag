Recipe: Homemade Ricotta Cheese Recipe

Description: This homemade ricotta cheese recipe is easy to make. All you need is four ingredients to make it from scratch and it will last for one week in the fridge.

Recipe category: ['Condiment']

Recipe cuisine: ['Italian']

Yield: ['3', '3 cups']

Cook time: PT20M

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 2 quarts whole milk
- 1 c heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 4 tablespoons fresh lemon juice

Instructions:
1. Heat milk, cream and salt in a large saucepan until it comes to a boil.
2. Add lemon juice
3. Lower heat and simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring to prevent burning to the bottom.
4. You will notice the curds forming.
5. Ladle curds into a cheesecloth lined sieve. I used paper towels.
6. Let sit for about 1 hour and strain.
7. Place cheese into a container and refrigerate for up to one week.
8. Throw out the remaining whey.

This homemade ricotta cheese recipe is easy to make. All you need is four ingredients to make it from scratch and it will last for one week in the fridge.
A few years ago I watched a show on TV about the Brooklyn market. One of the merchants stands in the market was run by a couple of women who made their own ricotta cheese. Ever since then, I've been wanting to try to come up with my own homemade ricotta cheese recipe.
How to Make Homemade Ricotta
I was really surprised at how easy it is to make ricotta cheese. Just four ingredients and some time.
This recipe calls for whole milk and cream. I'd like to try making it with 2 % milk and no cream, the next time because this one is on the rich side, but it sure tasted delicious.
Sometimes I make individual pizzas with spinach and ricotta . This homemade ricotta cheese recipe would be perfect for that application. Of course, it would be great in lasagna or manicotti. I also like it in pasta, direct with other veggies, in a garlic and olive oil sauce, or combined with a little red sauce.
Though store bought ricotta is certainly convenient, like many things in life, sometimes it's just fun to make from scratch.
Homemade Ricotta Cheese Recipe
Ingredients 1x 2x 3x
2 quarts whole milk
1 c heavy cream
1 teaspoon salt
4 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
Instructions
Heat milk, cream and salt in a large saucepan until it comes to a boil.
Add lemon juice
Lower heat and simmer for about 10 minutes, stirring to prevent burning to the bottom.
You will notice the curds forming.
Ladle curds into a cheesecloth lined sieve. I used paper towels.
Let sit for about 1 hour and strain.
Place cheese into a container and refrigerate for up to one week.
Throw out the remaining whey.
