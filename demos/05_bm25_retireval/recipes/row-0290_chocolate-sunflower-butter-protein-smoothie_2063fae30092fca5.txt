Recipe: Chocolate-Sunflower Butter Protein Smoothie

Description: This four ingredient smoothie with sunflower seed butter can satisfy a chocolate craving in a healthier way. Feel free to add ice if you want a thicker smoothie.

Recipe category: ['Breakfast', 'Drink']

Yield: ['1', '1 smoothie']

Prep time: PT5M

Total time: PT5M

Ingredients:
- 1 cup almond milk
- 1 scoop chocolate-flavored protein powder (such as FitMiss®)
- 2 tablespoons sunflower seed butter
- 2 teaspoons unsweetened cocoa powder

Instructions:
1. Pour almond milk into a blender. Add protein powder, sunflower seed butter, and cocoa powder. Blend until smooth, about 1 minute.
