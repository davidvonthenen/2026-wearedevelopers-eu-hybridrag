Recipe: Swiss Ham and Potato Soup

Description: This is an easy soup recipe that I've been making since the early 70's.

I love the flavors of the ham, swiss cheese and just a hint of nutmeg. This is a perfect soup for a chilly windy day.

This was a favorite for my daughter and now she makes it for her family too.

Hope you enjoy!

Recipe category: Cream Soups

Recipe cuisine: Swedish

Yield: Makes 4 1/2 cups or serves 3 people

Cook time: PT10M

Ingredients:
- 1 - 10 1/2-ounce can condensed cream of potato soup, undiluted
- 1 cup(s) half and half
- 3/4 cup(s) milk
- 1 1/2 cup(s) diced cooked ham
- 1/4 cup(s) shredded swiss cheese
- 1/8 teaspoon(s) nutmeg, ground

Instructions:
1. In a medium saucepan over low heat, heat all ingredients, stirring occasionally. Be careful to not over heat. Heat just until hot and cheese is melted. Serve topped with cheese and/or real bacon bits.
2. Note: Don't leave the nutmeg out, I believe this is a key ingredient for the wonderful flavor of this soup.
