Recipe: Oven-Fried Chicken

Description: I found this recipe in &quot;The Everything Cookbook&quot;. This has to be one of my DH &amp; I's favorite recipe for chicken. We have also used it for fish, substituting breadcrumbs for cereal crumbs.

Recipe category: Chicken Breast

Yield: 4-6 serving(s)

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT1H

Total time: PT1H15M

Ingredients:
- 6 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1/3 cup flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1 egg
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 cup finely crushed crisp rice cereal or 1 cup corn flakes
- 3 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
2. Place the flour, salt, pepper in a shallow dish/zipped baggie.
3. Beat egg lightly with water, place in seperate shallow dish.
4. Place crushed cereal crumbs in another shallow dish.
5. Rinse chicken.
6. Dip chicken in flour mix, coat evenly. Shake off excess flour.
7. Dip chicken in egg mix, coat evenly.
8. Roll chicken in cereal crumbs.
9. Place chicken on a cookie sheet that has been sprayed with non-fat cooking spray.
10. Drizzle with melted butter.
11. Bake 45 minutes - 1 hour.
12. Do not turn while baking.
