Recipe: Old Bay Fried Chicken

Description: OLD BAY Seasoning is the secret ingredient to this fabulous fried chicken that is sure to become a family favorite.

Recipe category: Entrees

Recipe cuisine: American

Yield: 6

Prep time: PT0H10M

Cook time: PT0H30M

Total time: PT0H40M

Ingredients:
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 cup flour
- 1/4 cup OLD BAY® Classic Seafood Seasoning
- 3 pounds chicken parts
- 2 cups vegetable oil

Instructions:
1. Beat egg and water in a large bowl. Mix flour and Old Bay in a plastic bag. Dip chicken in egg mixture; shake in flour mixture (a few pieces at a time). Discard any remaining flour mixture.
2. Heat oil in a large skillet on medium heat. Fry chicken for about 15 minutes per side or until golden brown. Drain on paper towels. If desired, sprinkle chicken with additional Old Bay.
