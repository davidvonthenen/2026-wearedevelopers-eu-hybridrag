Recipe: Salmon Mexican mess bowl

Description: Freshen up your nacho game with this vibrant, healthier salmon iteration.

Recipe category: dinner

Yield: serves 4

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 4x 140g Fresh Huon Salmon portions
- 440g cannellini beans, drained
- 1 teaspoon cumin, ground
- 2 cups passata
- 2 cups purple corn chips, crushed
- 1 avocado
- ½ cup sour cream

Instructions:
1. Heat a large fry-pan with the rice bran oil until hot. Add the cubed fresh Huon Salmon and gently cook for 1-2 minutes.
2. Add the fresh chilli and ground cumin
3. Add passata and cannellini beans and cook for a further 2-3 minutes
4. Serve with crushed purple corn chips, sour cream and slices of avocado
