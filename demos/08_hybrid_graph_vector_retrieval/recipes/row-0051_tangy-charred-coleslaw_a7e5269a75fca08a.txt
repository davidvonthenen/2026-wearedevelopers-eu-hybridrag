Recipe: Tangy Charred Coleslaw

Description: For this tangy charred coleslaw recipe, char your cabbage before dressing it with homemade vinaigrette. Once tossed, this 20-minute meal is a the perfect summer side dish.

Recipe category: ['vegetarian', 'picnic', 'salad', 'side dish']

Yield: 8 serving(s)

Prep time: PT0S

Cook time: PT0S

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 1/2 small green cabbage (about 11/4 lbs)
- 4 tbsp. olive oil, divided
- 2 tbsp. fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp. rice vinegar
- 1 tsp. brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp. red pepper flakes
- Kosher salt
- 1/2 Napa cabbage (about 11/4 lbs), cored and cut lengthwise, then into 1-in. pieces
- 1/4 c. fresh mint leaves, torn
- 1/4 c. toasted peanuts, chopped

Instructions:
1. Heat grill to medium-high. Cut green cabbage into 4 wedges, leaving core intact. Brush with 1 tablespoon oil, then place on grill flat side down and cook until charred, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
2. Meanwhile, in bowl, whisk together lime juice, vinegar, brown sugar, pepper flakes, remaining 3 tablespoons oil, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
3. Transfer green cabbage to cutting board and cut into 1-inch pieces. Add to bowl with dressing along with Napa cabbage and toss to coat; fold in mint and sprinkle with peanuts. <br/>
