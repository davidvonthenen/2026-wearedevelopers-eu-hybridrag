Recipe: Marinated Flank Steak

Description: Marinated Flank Steak (Margaret Price recipe).

Recipe category: Main Dish

Recipe cuisine: American

Yield: 6

Prep time: PT20M

Cook time: PT23H40M

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 2 Each Flank Steaks About 4 lbs
- 1 Cup Chili sauce or catsup
- 1/2 Cup Red Wine Vinegar
- 1 cup Burgundy Wine
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tablespoons Soy Sauce
- 2 tablespoons Grated yellow/white onion or use onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons Mustard (prepared)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup Olive oil

Instructions:
1. This steak is best prepared by marinating at least 6 hours - but preferably overnight. 1) Throughly mix marinade ingredients. 2) Pour a cup or so into the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish. Then add one flank steak and spoon marinade over it. Note: to tenderize each piece of steak a bit take a fork and force it into the meat on both sides repeatedly. 3) Pour some marinade over that, then add the second flank steak. 4) Pour the remainder of the marinade over the steaks. 5) Make sure that all parts of steaks are being marinated. 6) Cover with foil or plastic wrap and put in refrigerator. 7) Marinate for 6-24 hours, rotating steaks from top to bottom and flipping over at least 3 times during the marinating period. Grilling (this can also be broiled in an oven) This dish is served over rice, so get that started and coordinate finish time with the steak. 1) Preheat the grill or broiler. You want to sear this steak, not bake it. So very high heat. It will cook very quickly as it is thin and lean. 2) Do not overcook or the meat will toughen and lose flavor. The ends will be more well done than the middle, so those who like more well done can eat those portions. 3) Remove the steaks from the baking dish, scraping off the marinade back into the baking dish. 4) Pour the marinade into a small sauce pan and bring it to a boil, then back off so it simmers for at least 10 minutes. This will kill any bacteria from the raw meat. At the end of the cooking time check seasoning and adjust. 5) While the marinade is simmering, put the steak on the preheated grill and follow the instructions for grilling or broiling steak provided with that equipment. 6) Typically you will want to cook each side for 2-4 minutes depending on cooking circumstances. 7) Once done, remove to a meat carving surface and let sit for several minutes for the juices to set. 8) Carve against the grain (not lengthwise) into 1/2 - 3/4 inch strips. 9) Serve on top of rice 10) Ladle several tablespoons or more of cooked marinade over the steak and rice. Bon Appetit!

INSTRUCTIONS
This steak is best prepared by marinating at least 6 hours - but preferably overnight.
1) Throughly mix marinade ingredients.
2) Pour a cup or so into the bottom of a 9x13 baking dish. Then add one flank steak and spoon marinade over it. Note: to tenderize each piece of steak a bit take a fork and force it into the meat on both sides repeatedly.
3) Pour some marinade over that, then add the second flank steak.
4) Pour the remainder of the marinade over the steaks.
5) Make sure that all parts of steaks are being marinated.
6) Cover with foil or plastic wrap and put in refrigerator.
7) Marinate for 6-24 hours, rotating steaks from top to bottom and flipping over at least 3 times during the marinating period.
Grilling (this can also be broiled in an oven)
This dish is served over rice, so get that started and coordinate finish time with the steak.
1) Preheat the grill or broiler. You want to sear this steak, not bake it. So very high heat. It will cook very quickly as it is thin and lean.
2) Do not overcook or the meat will toughen and lose flavor. The ends will be more well done than the middle, so those who like more well done can eat those portions.
3) Remove the steaks from the baking dish, scraping off the marinade back into the baking dish.
4) Pour the marinade into a small sauce pan and bring it to a boil, then back off so it simmers for at least 10 minutes. This will kill any bacteria from the raw meat. At the end of the cooking time check seasoning and adjust.
5) While the marinade is simmering, put the steak on the preheated grill and follow the instructions for grilling or broiling steak provided with that equipment.
6) Typically you will want to cook each side for 2-4 minutes depending on cooking circumstances.
7) Once done, remove to a meat carving surface and let sit for several minutes for the juices to set.
8) Carve against the grain (not lengthwise) into 1/2 - 3/4 inch strips.
9) Serve on top of rice
10) Ladle several tablespoons or more of cooked marinade over the steak and rice.
Bon Appetit!
