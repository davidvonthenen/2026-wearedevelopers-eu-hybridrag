Ingredients Method
2 roma tomatoes, halved
1 small red onion, cut in wedges
1 small sweet potato, peeled and sliced
1 teaspoon olive oil
2 baguettes
basil leaves
100g feta cheese
Preheat the oven to 180°C. Place the tomatoes, onion and sweet potato on to a baking tray and drizzle with the oil. Cook for 25 minutes, then cool slightly. Fill the baguettes with the vegetables, basil leaves and feta.
