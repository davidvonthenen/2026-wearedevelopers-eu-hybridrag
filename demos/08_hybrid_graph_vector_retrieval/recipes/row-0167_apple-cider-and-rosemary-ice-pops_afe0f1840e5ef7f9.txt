Recipe: Apple Cider and Rosemary Ice Pops

Description: Apple Cider and Rosemary Ice Pops Recipe

Yield: makes 12 Servings

Ingredients:
- 4 cups apple juice
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken in half
- 2 whole cloves
- 4 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:
1. Combine first 6 ingredients in saucepan. Bring mixture to boil, stirring until sugar dissolves. Reduce heat to medium; simmer until mixture is reduced to 3 1/2 cups, 20 to 25 minutes. Strain into 4-cup measuring cup; cool to room temperature. Stir in vinegar and vanilla. Pour into twelve 1/3-cup ice pop molds. Insert ice pop covers and sticks; freeze overnight.
2. Small paper cups can be used instead of ice pop molds. Pour the mixture into the paper cups, partially freeze, then insert a wooden stick into the center of each and freeze overnight. If pops are difficult to remove from cups, cut a slit in each cup with scissors, then peel it off.
