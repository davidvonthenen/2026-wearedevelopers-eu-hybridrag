Description
This elegant, luxurious, and vegan-friendly dessert is perfect to wow a crowd at any party!
Ingredients
2 whole Large Ripe Avocados
½ cups Unsweetened Cocoa Powder
4 Tablespoons Honey
2 teaspoons Vanilla Extract
1 pinch Salt
1 Tablespoon Brewed Coffee (optional)
Preparation
Discard avocado seeds and scoop flesh into food processor. Add all other ingredients and blend until smooth and creamy. Divide into four 6-ounce ramekins and chill for 1 hour. Serve chilled.
