Recipe: Chunky Capsicum and Cashew Dip

Description: Make and share this Chunky Capsicum and Cashew Dip recipe from Food.com.

Recipe category: Peppers

Yield: 1 1/3 cups

Prep time: PT10M

Total time: PT10M

Ingredients:
- 150 g marinated roasted red peppers, drained (capsicum)
- 150 g unsalted dry-roasted cashews
- 1/4 cup fresh parmesan cheese, finely grated
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

Instructions:
1. Process all ingredients in a food processor until well combined but not completely smooth.
2. Season well with fresh ground black pepper and serve with crackers.
