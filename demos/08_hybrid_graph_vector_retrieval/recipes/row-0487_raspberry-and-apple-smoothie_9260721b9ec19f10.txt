Recipe: Raspberry and apple smoothie

Description: Whip up a breakfast smoothie with raspberries, apples, yogurt and oats. It’s a great way to start the day and up your fruit intake

Recipe category: Breakfast, Drink

Yield: Makes 4 (150ml)

Prep time: PT5M

Total time: PT5M

Ingredients:
- 2 apples cored (we used Granny Smith)
- 150g frozen raspberries
- 150ml natural yogurt
- 2 tbsp porridge oats
- ½ lemon juiced
- 100ml milk

Instructions:
1. Tip all ingredients into a blender or smoothie maker and blitz until smooth, adding 50ml water or milk if it’s too thick.
