How to make it
1.Spread peanut butter between 2 cheese crackers.
2.Melt Chocolate Candiquik coating according to directions on package.
3.Dip cracker/peanut butter combination in chocolate Candiquik, let excess chocolate run off, and place on wax paper to dry.
Optional: sprinkle crushed cheese crackers on top to garnish.
