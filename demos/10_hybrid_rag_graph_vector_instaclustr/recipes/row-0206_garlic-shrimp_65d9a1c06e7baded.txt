Recipe: Garlic Shrimp

Description: I call this my magic shrimp. You can have this done in 21 minutes. And it's true restaurant quality.

Recipe category: Vegetable

Yield: 4 serving(s)

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT6M

Total time: PT21M

Ingredients:
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 4 tablespoons minced garlic
- 1 1/2 lbs fresh shrimp, peeled and deviened
- 1 cup julienned carrot
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro

Instructions:
1. In 12in skillet, heat oil.
2. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
3. Add shrimp, stir fry 1 minute.
4. Add carrot, stir fry 3 more minutes.
5. Stir in cilantro.
