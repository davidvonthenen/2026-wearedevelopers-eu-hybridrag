Recipe: Vitamix Whipped Cream

Recipe category: dessert

Yield: 2 c (240 ml)

Prep time: P0Y0M0DT0H10M0.000S

Total time: P0Y0M0DT0H10M0.000S

Ingredients:
- 2 cups (480 ml) heavy whipping cream, cold
- 3 tablespoons (38 g) sugar or other sweetener, to taste (optional)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions:
1. 1. Place all ingredients into the Vitamix container and secure lid.
2. 2. Select Variable 1.
3. 3. Turn machine on and slowly increase speed to Variable 10, then to High.
4. 4. Blend for 12 seconds for soft peaks. Do not over mix. Serve within two hours.
