Recipe: Spicy Strawberry Salad

Description: In this spicy strawberry salad recipe, mushrooms, salad greens, pecans, and lots of fresh, sliced strawberries are dressed with a poppy seed dressing.

Recipe category: ['Lunch']

Recipe cuisine: ['American']

Yield: 10

Prep time: PT20M

Total time: PT20M

Ingredients:
- 0.75 cup vegetable oil
- 0.5 cup white sugar
- 0.33333334326744 cup balsamic vinegar
- 1 tablespoon poppy seeds
- 1.5 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon mustard powder
- 8 cups mixed salad greens
- 1.5 cups fresh sliced mushrooms
- 4 cups sliced fresh strawberries
- 1.5 cups chopped pecans

Instructions:
1. Whisk oil, sugar, vinegar, poppy seeds, garlic powder, and dry mustard together in a bowl. Refrigerate until chilled.
2. Combine mixed greens and mushrooms in a salad bowl; add vinaigrette and toss to coat. Sprinkle with strawberries and pecans.
