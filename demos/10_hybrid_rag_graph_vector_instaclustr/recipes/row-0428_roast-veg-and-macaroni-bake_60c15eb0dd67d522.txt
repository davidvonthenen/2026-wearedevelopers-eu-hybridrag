Recipe: Roast Veg and Macaroni Bake

Description: not set

Recipe category: Main Dish

Recipe cuisine: not set

Yield: 2

Prep time: PT30M

Cook time: PT30M

Total time: PT30M

Ingredients:
- 1 carrot peeled and chopped into bite size peices
- 1 small parsnip dito
- 1 small pumpkin dito
- 4 medium brussel sprouts chopped into thirds
- 2 rashers bacon diced
- 1 cup macaroni pasta
- 2 tablespoons cheese spread
- 1 tablespoon Margarine
- 10 - 15 mls sweet chilli sauce
- breadcrumbs for topping
- Margarine for topping

Instructions:
1. place cut carrot, parsnip and pumpkin in a small plastic bag with approx. 5 mls oil, toss until completely covered. place the vegies into a small casserole dish and cook in oven at 180 degrees for approx. 15 mins til vegies have started to brown. add the Brussel sprouts and bacon, to the vegies and toss. place back into oven for approx. another 15 to 30 minutes. While vegies are cooking cook the pasta. When cooked drain and place back into pot . Add the sweet chilli sauce, cheese spread, and milk and cook til cheese spread has melted. Poor over vegies, toss and then top with breadcrumbs and margarine which have been combined, and sprinkle over top and place in oven til browned.
