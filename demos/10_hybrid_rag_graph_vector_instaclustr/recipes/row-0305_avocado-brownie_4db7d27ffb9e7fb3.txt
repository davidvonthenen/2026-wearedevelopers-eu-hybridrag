Description
This healthier version of brownies replaces butter with avocado, making it lighter but no less tasty. The brownie will still be deliciously fudgy!
Ingredients
2 whole Eggs
½ cups White Sugar
½ cups Brown Sugar
¼ cups Flour
½ cups Cocoa Powder, Unsweetened
1 teaspoon Vanilla Extract
¼ teaspoons Sea Salt
1 whole Avocado
¼ cups Walnuts
¼ cups Cocoa Nibs
Preparation
Preheat oven to 400ºF.
Mix eggs and sugars together in a bowl. Add flour, cocoa powder, vanilla and salt and mix until combined.
Mix in avocado flesh until you have a smooth, lump-free mixture. Crush walnuts and stir them into the mixture along with the cocoa nibs.
Grease a brownie pan or line with baking paper. Pour the mixture into the pan and spread out evenly. Bake for 30 minutes.
Enjoy!
