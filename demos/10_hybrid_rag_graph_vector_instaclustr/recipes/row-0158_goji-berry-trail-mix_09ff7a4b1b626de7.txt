Recipe: Goji Berry Trail Mix

Description: Goji Berries add a new twist to trail mix, &amp; with substitutions &amp;/or additions or nuts, dried fruits &amp; small pretzels, etc, the results can be endless.

- 1 cup dark raisin
- 1 cup sunflower seeds
- 1 cup sliced almonds
