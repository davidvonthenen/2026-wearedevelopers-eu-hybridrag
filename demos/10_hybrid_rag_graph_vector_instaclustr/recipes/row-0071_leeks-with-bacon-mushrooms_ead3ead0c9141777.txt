Recipe: Leeks with bacon & mushrooms

Description: A delicious, versatile side dish of sweet braised leeks and crispy bacon - great with a Sunday roast

Recipe category: Lunch, Side dish

Recipe cuisine: British

Yield: 4

Prep time: PT15M

Cook time: PT30M

Total time: PT45M

Ingredients:
- 50g butter
- 4 rashers smoked streaky bacon chopped
- few thyme sprigs
- 200g pack chestnut mushroom quartered, or halved
- 2 leeks thickly sliced

Instructions:
1. Melt the butter in a large frying pan, then add the bacon and fry for a few mins until golden. Add the thyme, mushrooms and leeks with seasoning (remember bacon can be salty), cover pan and cook on a low heat for 20-25 mins until leeks are tender.
