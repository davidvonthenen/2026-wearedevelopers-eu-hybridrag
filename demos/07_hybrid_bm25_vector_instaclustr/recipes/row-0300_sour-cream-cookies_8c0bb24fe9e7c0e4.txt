Recipe: Sour Cream Cookies

Description: Wow!

Recipe category: Desserts

Recipe cuisine: not set

Yield: 48

Prep time: PT30M

Cook time: PT3H30M

Total time: PT4H

Ingredients:
- 3 Eggs
- 2 cups Sugar
- 1 cup Butter softened
- 1 cup Sour cream
- 2 tsp Baking soda
- pinch Salt
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 5 Cups All purpose flour

Instructions:
1. Mix together the eggs, sugar, butter and sour cream. Add vanilla. Mix salt and soda with 2 cups of the flour. Mix in. Then add enough flour to handle the dough (2-3 cups). Dough will be sticky. Chill 2-3 hours or overnight. Roll dough to 3/8"thickness and cut shapes. Bake at 350° for 10-12 minutes or until slightly golden. Cool, ice and decorate.
