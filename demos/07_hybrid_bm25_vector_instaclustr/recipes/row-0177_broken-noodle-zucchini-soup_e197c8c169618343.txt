Recipe Text From URL
--------------------
If you think 16 cups of soup is a lot for four people, well, that’s the beauty of low calorie-density foods. So enjoy and eat heartily!
Ingredients
1 medium - onion, chopped
1 medium - poblano chili pepper, seeded and diced
2 Tbs - dried oregano
8 cups - water
4 medium - zucchini, chopped
1 16 oz pkg - frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn, and green beans)
1 16 oz pkg - whole-wheat fettuccini or spaghetti, broken in quarters
Instructions
In a soup pot over medium heat, sauté the onion, poblano pepper, and dried oregano until the vegetables are tender, about 3 minutes. Add a little water as needed to prevent sticking.
Add the water, zucchini, mixed vegetables, and spaghetti noodles, cover, and cook for about 15 minutes, until the pasta is tender. Salt to taste.
Nutrition Information
Nutrition (per serving): 507 calories, 78% calories from carbohydrates, 15% calories from protein, 8% calories from fat, 19g fiber.
