Luby's Cafeteria Carrot Raisin Salad
add review
#77122
ingredients
2 pounds carrots, peeled and finely shredded 1 cup drained crushed pineapple 1 cup mayonnaise 1 cup raisins 1/4 cup powdered sugar
directions
Combine the carrots, pineapple, mayonnaise, raisins, and sugar in a bowl. Mix well to combine. Adjust the flavor as needed with more sugar if desired. Cover the bowl and place in the refrigerator for 1-2 hours before serving.
