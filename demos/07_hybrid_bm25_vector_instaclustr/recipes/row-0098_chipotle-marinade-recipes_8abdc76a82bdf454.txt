Recipe: Chipotle Marinade Recipe

Yield: ['1', '1 1/2 pounds meat']

Prep time: PT10M

Total time: PT10M

Ingredients:
- 4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons cider vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions:
1. In a large zip top bag combine all ingredients.
2. Add steak and marinate in the fridge for 4 to 6 hours. Never leave uncooked marinating meat at room temperature for more than 40 minutes.

Visible page text:
Chipotle Marinade Recipe
Marinating is a good way to get more flavor and tenderness from your meat, which is especially important when you are grilling, because of the quick cooking time. In this Chipotle Marinade Recipe , the vinegar helps to tenderize the meat, while the chipotle peppers infuse it with smoky spice.
Ingredients
4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
3 tablespoons olive oil
3 tablespoons cider vinegar
1/4 teaspoon salt
Directions
In a large zip top bag combine all ingredients.
Add steak and marinate in the fridge for 4 to 6 hours. Never leave uncooked marinating meat at room temperature for more than 40 minutes.
