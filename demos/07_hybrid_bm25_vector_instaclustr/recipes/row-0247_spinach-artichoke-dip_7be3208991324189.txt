Description
One of the best homemade spinach artichoke dips I’ve had, and easy to boot!
Ingredients
3 Tablespoons Butter
1 whole Medium Onion, Chopped
2 cloves Garlic, Minced
1 package (10 Oz. Package) Frozen Spinach
1 can (13 3/4 Oz. Can) Artichoke Hearts
8 ounces, weight Cream Cheese
½ cups Mayonnaise
¾ cups Parmesan Cheese
1 package (8 Oz. Package) 4 Cheese Blend
2 bags Pita Chips, Bruschetta Chips, Etc. To Serve
Preparation
Melt the butter; add onion and garlic. Saute until tender. Add spinach and cook for 3 minutes over medium heat, stirring often.
Add artichoke hearts, cream cheese, mayonnaise, Parmesan cheese, and 4 cheese blend; stir until blended.
Spoon into a greased baking dish that will double as a serving piece.
Bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes, stirring often.
If desired, add mozzarella on top and bake for 5-10 minutes more.
