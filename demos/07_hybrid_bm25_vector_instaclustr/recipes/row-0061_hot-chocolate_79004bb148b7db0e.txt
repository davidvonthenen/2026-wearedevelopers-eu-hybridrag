Recipe: Hot Chocolate

Recipe category: beverage

Yield: 4 servings

Prep time: P0Y0M0DT0H5M0.000S

Cook time: P0Y0M0DT0H8M0.000S

Total time: P0Y0M0DT0H13M0.000S

Ingredients:
- 4 cups milk
- 4 tablespoons Dutch process cocoa, mixed into a paste with 1/4 cup milk
- 4 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 ounces peppermint schnapps
- 1 pint heavy cream, whipped
- Grated bittersweet chocolate for garnish
- 4 candy canes

Instructions:
1. In a small saucepan, over low heat, heat milk. Stir in cocoa mixed with milk, sugar and continue to stir until cocoa is incorporated and sugar is dissolved. Do not bring mixture to a boil. Off heat add peppermint Schnapps. Pour hot chocolate into 4 mugs. Top with whipped cream, grated chocolate and add 1 candy cane per mug.
