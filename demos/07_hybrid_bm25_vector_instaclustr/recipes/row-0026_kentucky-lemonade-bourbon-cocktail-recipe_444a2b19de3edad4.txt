Recipe: Kentucky Lemonade – Bourbon Cocktail Recipe

Description: This Kentucky Lemonade Cocktail Recipe is the perfect cocktail recipe for the summer!

Ingredients:
- 1 1/2 ounces of Bourbon (I used Weller Special Reserve)
- 1/2 ounce of Triple Sec
- 1/2 cup of Lemonade
- a generous splash of lemon-lime soda

Instructions:
1. Pour the bourbon, triple sec and lemonade into a shaker and shake well. Pour the mixture over crushed ice and add a splash of lemon-lime soda. Garnish with a lemon slice, and enjoy!

Kentucky Lemonade – Bourbon Cocktail Recipe
Ingredients
1 ½ ounces of Bourbon I used Weller Special Reserve
½ ounce of Triple Sec
½ cup of Lemonade
a generous splash of lemon-lime soda
Instructions
Pour the bourbon, triple sec and lemonade into a shaker and shake well. Pour the mixture over crushed ice and add a splash of lemon-lime soda. Garnish with a lemon slice, and enjoy!
