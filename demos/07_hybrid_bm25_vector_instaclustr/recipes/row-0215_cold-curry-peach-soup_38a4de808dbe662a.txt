Description
Nice and spicy, great with grilled chicken or as an appetizer.
Ingredients
6 whole Peaches Peeled And Pitted
½ whole Jalapeno Roughly Chopped (seeded If You Prefer Less Heat)
1 clove Garlic, Roughly Chopped
2 Tablespoons Grated Ginger
2 teaspoons Curry Powder
1 pinch Cardamom
¼ teaspoons Garam Masala
½ cups Coconut Milk
¼ cups Water
Salt And Pepper
Preparation
Place all ingredients into a blender or food processor. Blend until smooth. If it’s too thick, add more water as needed.
Serve room temperature or cold.
