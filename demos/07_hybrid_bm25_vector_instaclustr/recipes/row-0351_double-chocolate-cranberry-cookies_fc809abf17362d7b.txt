Recipe: Double Chocolate Cranberry Cookies

Description: Perfect for holiday cookie gifting, these double chocolate cookies have the perfect balance of tart and sweet, thanks to dried cranberries and semisweet chocolate chips.

Recipe category: ['Dessert']

Yield: ['72', '72 cookies']

Prep time: PT20M

Cook time: PT15M

Total time: PT40M

Ingredients:
- 2.25 cups all-purpose flour
- 0.5 cup unsweetened cocoa
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 0.5 cup white sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1.5 cups dried cranberries
- 1.5 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease two cookie sheets.
2. Sift flour, cocoa, baking soda, and salt together in a bowl.
3. Cream butter, brown sugar, and white sugar together with an electric mixer in a large bowl. Add eggs, one at a time, mixing after each addition. Mix in vanilla extract. Gradually add flour mixture until combined. Mix in cranberries and chocolate chips.
4. Place rounded tablespoonfuls of dough 2 inches apart on the prepared cookie sheets. Flatten each ball slightly with a fork.
5. Bake in the preheated oven until cookies are soft and tops are no longer glossy, 12 to 14 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes on the cookie sheet, then remove to a cooling rack to cool completely.

Cook's Notes:
Any dried fruit can be used in place of the dried cranberries, just make sure any large pieces of dried fruit get cut up to about the size of a raisin.
White chocolate chips also work in place of the semisweet.
